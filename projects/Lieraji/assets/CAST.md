## Two readings that catch people out

- **坂倉花 is Sakakura _Sakura_**, not Hana. 花 is normally *hana*; here it reads
  *sakura*. Do not "correct" it.
- **大熊和奏 is _Okuma_ Wakana** on the official page — no doubled vowel. Ōkuma /
  Ookuma are valid transliterations but are not what the official site prints.
