### Story Synopsis & Character Arc
1. **Reuniting with Shiho's Origin (Episodes 1–3)**:
   Fresh off their first professional one-man live, Shiho attends an arena concert by the popular veteran girl band **Iolite** and is deeply moved by their bassist, **Sumire** (Kuno). Soon after, Leo/need's manager **Shindo** brings them a major break: their national TV debut on the hit music program **`WAVING-BOX`**, co-starring as the newcomer spotlight band alongside Iolite. While filling out the pre-show questionnaire (`今の楽器を始めた理由` — "Why I started my instrument"), Shiho flashes back to elementary school, when Sumire gently taught her how to pluck bass strings with two fingers and told her how happy she looked holding the instrument—inspiring Shiho's lifelong resolve to stay true to her convictions (`あんなふうに……自分の想いを貫けるようになりたい`).
2. **Preparation & Unexpected Bass Duel (Episodes 4–6)**:
   While Saki leads mock talk-show rehearsals in the School SEKAI (with advice from Luka, Rin, and the other Virtual Singers) to help the camera-shy band prepare, the `WAVING-BOX` producer reads Shiho's questionnaire and proposes a live televised **bassist battle** between Iolite's Sumire and Leo/need's Shiho. Determined not to hold back in front of her idol, Shiho spars intensely on bass with **Megurine Luka** in the SEKAI. On broadcast day, despite endearing rookie blunders during the talk segment (Ichika sitting in the wrong chair, Saki slipping on last names), Leo/need's sincerity wins over host **HAYASE** and the studio audience.
3. **Climax — "The Road Here" (Episode 7)**:
   Standing across from Sumire with nothing between them but their basses, Shiho pours all her gratitude, respect, and years of devotion into her sound (`ずっと、どんな時も……ベースだけは手放さなかった / 絶対、自分の想いを貫くって決めたから——だから、今、ここにいるんだ！`). Sumire answers at full strength, leaving the studio breathless. Afterward, when Shiho says she still has a long way to go before her bass can move Sumire's heart, Sumire smiles and replies, *"It reached me a long time ago"*—revealing she secretly attended a Leo/need concert after hearing about Shiho's band from Shiho's father, and even recognized Leo/need's debut song playing in town because the bass carried such unmistakable, unbending conviction (`絶対に曲げない……曲げられない信念があるって感じさせてくれる音`).
4. **Resolution — "Resonating, Spreading Out" (Episode 8)**:
   `WAVING-BOX` trends on social media, driving a surge in MV views, job offers, and individual fan mail. Reading the letters together in the SEKAI, Shiho opens one addressed to her:
   > *「番組で日野森さんの演奏を聴いて、私もベースを始めようと思いました。全力でベースを弾く日野森さんの姿がすごく楽しそうで、かっこよくて。音が、体の真ん中に響いてくるみたいで。私も、あんな風になりたいと思ったんです」*
   > (*"After hearing Hinomori-san play on the show, I decided I want to start playing bass too. Seeing you play with everything you had looked so joyful and cool—like the sound was resonating straight into my chest. It made me think, 'I want to become like that too.'"*)
   Just as Sumire once planted that seed in young Shiho (`Ep 1: いつか、あんな風に`), Shiho has now passed that exact spark on to someone new—quietly affirming that her small, steady steps alongside Ichika, Saki, and Honami are real (`一歩一歩は、すごく小さいかもしれない。けど——少しずつでも、進んでいけてる`).

### How the Interview Connects Lyrics, Sound, and Story
| Timestamp | Interview Topic | Lyric / Story Resonance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `22:07–22:55` | **Title Origin** | Colorful Palette's commission brief (`依頼書`/`企画書`) explicitly gave `「このままいこう」` (*"let's keep going as we are"*) as the song's key phrase. KEI immediately felt that line worked as-is for both lyrics and title, softening it colloquially into `「このまんまでいこう」` (*"Going On as We Are"*). |
| `22:58–24:44` | **Live 5-Piece Band & *Hikitemita* Challenge** | KEI wanted raw band energy and youthful freshness (`瑞々しさ`), structuring the arrangement strictly around a 5-piece live band (drums, bass, piano, 2 guitars—matching Leo/need + Virtual Singer) recorded by **live studio musicians** rather than MIDI (`打ち込み`). At vocal recording, KEI told Yuki he refused to water down the instrumental parts just because the characters are high schoolers—throwing down a playful gauntlet (`弾けるかい？`) to *hikitemita* (`弾いてみた`) instrumental cover players, mirroring Shiho's uncompromising pro standard in Ep 5 & Ep 7. |
| `24:44–28:25` | **Lyric Philosophy: Clumsy Perseverance Over Hollow "Dreams Come True"** | KEI writes lyrics *before* melody and refuses to write lies (`嘘っぱち`) against his own 40 years of life, having seen dreams fail and peers quit. Overlapping Leo/need's arc (hesitating, stopping, yet choosing to keep playing together) with his own life of continuing music for small, ordinary reasons, he wrote `"Going On as We Are"` not about chasing/achieving dreams, but **"the story of people who didn't quit"** (`何かをやめなかった人の物語`), praising **"the clumsiness of being unable to throw a dream away"** (`夢を捨てられない不器用さを称える歌` → Verse 1 `くしゃくしゃになっても 捨てられないでいる` & Ep 7 `ベースだけは手放さなかった`). |
| `28:25–31:12` | **Yuki's KEI Fandom & Shiho's Smile** | Yuki grew up singing KEI's 2013 track **"dialogue"** (`ダイアログ`) at karaoke and was thrilled when Leo/need covered **"Hello, Worker"** in 2023, calling it "fate" to receive a Shiho focus song from him. She notes how rare it is to see Shiho smiling so openly in an MV—purely radiating *"I love music and want to keep going with everyone in Leo/need!"* |
| `31:12–32:45` | **KEI's Favorite Lyric: Post-2nd-Chorus Bridge** | KEI highlights `「虹のかからない雨上がりに それでも種は土に芽吹く」` (*"Even after a rain with no rainbow, seeds still sprout in the soil"*). Rain doesn't guarantee a rainbow, and hardship doesn't guarantee flashy success—yet ordinary life quietly continuing (`淡々と営みが続いていく`) holds beauty and worth, and unseen seeds still sprout. In Ep 8, that unseen seed literally sprouts when a viewer writes a fan letter saying Shiho inspired them to pick up the bass. |
| `32:46–34:08` | **Vocal Recording Direction** | Usually Shiho sings and speaks coolly, so Yuki intentionally sang in one of her brightest tones ever—letting *"I love this sound!"* (`音が好きです`) and an audible smile shine through while preserving Shiho's cool rock edge. |
