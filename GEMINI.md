# Antigravity Workspace Guidelines

## Character Rules & Conventions
- **Mizuki Akiyama (暁山瑞希)**: Always use "she/her" pronouns (never "they/them").

## Style Conventions
- **Names**: Western order ("Given Family", e.g., "Mizuki Akiyama").
- **Song/event titles**: Quoted in double quotes, not italicized.
- **Project Sekai terms**: "AfterTalk" (capital T), "AfterLive" (capital L), "ProSeka" (capital S).
- **Contractions**: Always use natural spoken forms.
- **Line length**: Subtitles must never exceed 2 rows on screen.
